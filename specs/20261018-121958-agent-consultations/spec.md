# Feature Specification: Delegated Coach-to-Specialist Consultations

**Feature Branch**: `20261018-121958-agent-consultations`

**Created**: 2026-10-18

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "Delegated Coach-to-Specialist Consultations: the architecture defines Hermes-orchestrated `CA -> SA` consultations: a Coach Agent may autonomously consult an allowed Specialist; the question, response, evidence, and influence on the Coach's synthesis remain visible and attributed; a consultation creates a child logical invocation identified by `consultation_id` that links it to the parent CA invocation and inherits the initiating actor, stamp, team, match, conversation, correlation, route, and tool-policy context; its response returns only to the invoking CA; `CA -> CA`, `SA -> SA`, and SA-initiated `SA -> CA` requests are rejected before executable child work is created; the parent moves through `waiting-for-consultations` and `synthesizing`; parent waiting edges and accepted child outcomes survive restarts; a child that exhausts its retry budget is recorded as an attributed unavailable consultation and the parent continues or fails according to its versioned orchestration policy, never through an unrecorded fallback; authorization is re-evaluated at delegation; and Wave 4 enables consultation after context inheritance, attribution, conflict preservation, loop prevention, latency, and cost gates (docs/architecture/intelligence-and-agents.md#agent-orchestration-authority, #durable-state-and-transaction-boundaries, #client-transport-and-runtime-protocol, #authorization-lineage-and-lifecycle, #post-analysis-match-triage; docs/architecture/intelligence-agent-catalog.md Interaction Model, Implementation Waves, Validation And Promotion Gates, the Coach drafts' consultation guidance). This feature implements consultations end to end: Agent Orchestration accepts a consultation request from the current attempt of a Coach Agent invocation (attempt- and fencing-bound, idempotent per request), validates the route and that the Specialist is allowed for that Coach (the plan decides where the allowed-Specialist list lives, preferably in the Coach's pinned catalog artifacts), creates the child invocation with inherited context and pinned Specialist artifacts, moves the parent to `waiting-for-consultations` and later `synthesizing`, bounds the number and depth of consultations per parent (no nested consultations), delivers accepted child outcomes or attributed unavailable outcomes to the parent, and preserves conflicts between the Coach's synthesis and the Specialist's advice in lineage; Hermes lets a Coach Agent request consultations through a Hermes-local function within those bounds, executes the child as a normal Specialist invocation, resumes the parent with the child outcomes, and restores the consultation instruction to the seeded Coach prompts; members' reads and the triage Web UI show each consultation's question, response, evidence, attribution, and influence on the synthesis. Automated tests use Hermes' scripted provider and the cross-process setup to prove routing rules, inheritance, restart recovery, retry exhaustion, loop prevention, and attribution; Wave 4 evaluation gates (latency, cost, conflict preservation quality) stay required production evidence and are not claimed. Out of scope: SA-initiated or CA-to-CA interactions (rejected), nested consultations, parallel multi-turn negotiation, evaluation suites, real provider calls in tests, production values. Depends on `specs/20261018-115720-coach-agent-conversations` (batch sibling), `specs/20261018-113729-hermes-runtime`, `specs/archive/20261018-101517-agent-catalog-registry`, `specs/archive/20261017-204332-agent-orchestration-invocations` (#155), `specs/archive/20261017-210231-web-ui-triage-conversations` (#156). Implements the consultation parts of the sections named above (Wave 4 functionality without its evaluation gates)."

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-18

- Q: What happens to the Coach Agent attempt when the platform refuses its consultation request for a reason other than a stale disposition? → A: Hermes returns the refusal reason to the model as the consultation function's result, records a content-free checkpoint naming the reason, and the attempt continues; stale dispositions and scope loss stop the attempt as for any other operation.
- Q: How many consultations per parent invocation and which unavailable behavior do the Development and Test seed's consultation policies use? → A: At most 3 consultations per parent invocation, and the parent continues when a consultation is unavailable (development values).
- Q: Which execution limits govern a Coach Agent invocation whose version may consult, given that its attempt waits while its children run? → A: A separate versioned execution policy for consulting parents, recorded at submission, whose maximum attempt duration covers waiting for consultations; children use their own versioned child execution policy; both have development values.
- Q: What may the owner see of a consultation before the Coach Agent's invocation has ended? → A: The consulted Specialist, the question, and the consultation's state; the Specialist's response and evidence become visible once the parent invocation has ended (succeeded or failed), because the response returns only to the invoking Coach Agent.
- Q: Which endings of a child invocation make its consultation unavailable? → A: Attempts exhausted and every non-retryable failure (including invalid or incompatible artifacts and an unsupported route reported by the runtime); scope loss and the parent ending never do.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

The actors are the Coach (or Club Admin) who owns a triage conversation and asks a Coach Agent for a whole-match perspective, the Club Admin who governs which Specialist Agents a Coach Agent version may consult, and the Hermes agent runtime that executes the Coach Agent and the Specialist Agents it consults. A delegated consultation is the Hermes-orchestrated `CA -> SA` path of [Supported Interactions](../../docs/architecture/intelligence-and-agents.md#supported-interactions): the Coach Agent asks one allowed Specialist Agent one bounded question, the Specialist answers only to that Coach Agent, and the Coach Agent synthesizes the answer for the human coach without hiding disagreement ([Interaction Model](../../docs/architecture/intelligence-agent-catalog.md#interaction-model)). Conversations, invocations, attempts, leases, fencing, stale dispositions, retries, scope loss, accepted advice, and lineage come from [Agent Orchestration Direct Invocations](../archive/20261017-204332-agent-orchestration-invocations/spec.md); agent versions, artifacts, pins, and the attempt-scoped artifact read from the [Agent Catalog Registry](../archive/20261018-101517-agent-catalog-registry/spec.md); direct Coach Agent conversations, profile rendering, modifiers, disclosure, and the Coach Agent seed from [Coach Agent Direct Conversations](../20261018-115720-coach-agent-conversations/spec.md), which offers Coach Agents no consultation yet; execution from the [Hermes Agent Runtime](../20261018-113729-hermes-runtime/spec.md); evidence from the [MCP Server](../20261018-111804-mcp-server/spec.md) and the [Agent Evidence Tools API](../20261018-104924-agent-evidence-tools/spec.md); members use [Web UI Match Triage Conversations](../archive/20261017-210231-web-ui-triage-conversations/spec.md) and Club Admins [Web UI Agent Catalog Administration](../20261018-103316-web-ui-agent-catalog/spec.md).

### User Story 1 - A Coach Agent consults a Specialist and synthesizes the answer (Priority: P1)

A Coach asks `coach-jurgen-klopp` why the team faded after the hour mark. While answering, the Coach Agent decides that the tactics specialist should check whether the pressing structure broke down, and asks it a bounded question. The platform records the consultation, creates the Specialist's child invocation with the conversation's context, and the parent waits. Hermes executes the child as an ordinary Specialist invocation with its own evidence calls; its answer returns only to the Coach Agent, which completes its synthesis and declares how the consultation influenced it. The Coach reads the Coach Agent's advice together with the consultation's question, the Specialist's response and evidence, and whether the Coach Agent adopted or disagreed with it.

**Why this priority**: It delivers Wave 4's delegated consultation end to end; every other story hardens or exposes this path.

**Independent Test**: With the Development and Test seed and Hermes' scripted provider (scripted so that the Coach Agent calls `get_match`, requests one consultation of `specialist-tactics`, and answers after the result, and the Specialist calls `find_pressing_phases` and answers), submit a question to the Coach Agent and confirm that one consultation and one child invocation exist with the inherited context and the Specialist's pins, that the parent passed through waiting for consultations and synthesizing, that the parent's advice is accepted with a declared influence for the consultation, and that the owner's reads show the consultation's question, response, the Specialist's tool call, and the attribution.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an active attempt of a direct Coach Agent invocation whose pinned Coach Agent version allows consulting `specialist-tactics`, and an enabled `specialist-tactics`, **When** the attempt requests a consultation with a bounded question, the evidence it already holds, and the decision support it wants, **Then** the platform, in one atomic step, records the consultation linked to the parent, creates the child invocation queued for the Specialist with the parent's initiating actor, team, match, run, conversation, and correlation and the Specialist's enabled version and artifacts pinned, records the parent's waiting edge, moves the parent to waiting for consultations, and publishes a work notification for the child.
2. **Given** a queued child invocation, **When** Hermes claims it, **Then** it executes it as a Specialist invocation of the consultation route with its own attempt, lease, fencing token, evidence session, and the Specialist's pinned tool policy, and completes it with advice that follows the shared response contract.
3. **Given** a completed child, **When** the platform accepts its outcome, **Then** the child succeeds with exactly one accepted response, the parent's waiting edge is resolved, and, once every consultation of the parent is resolved, the parent moves to synthesizing.
4. **Given** a parent attempt waiting for its consultations, **When** their outcomes are accepted, **Then** Hermes resumes the Coach Agent with each consultation's attributed response, and the Coach Agent completes with advice and a declaration, for every consultation, of whether it adopted, partially adopted, or did not adopt the response, whether it agrees or conflicts, and a short note.
5. **Given** a succeeded parent, **When** its owner reads the conversation turns, the invocation, or its lineage, **Then** each consultation is shown attributed to the consulted Specialist with its question, response, evidence tool calls, state, and the Coach Agent's declared influence and any conflict, and the child's response appears nowhere as a turn of its own.

### User Story 2 - Only allowed, bounded, non-nested consultations are created (Priority: P2)

Consultation is a delegation of authority, so it is governed: only a Coach Agent attempt may consult, only Specialists its pinned Coach Agent version allows, only within a bound per parent invocation, never from a child, never to a Coach Agent, and never with stale authority. Every refused request leaves no executable child work.

**Why this priority**: Loop prevention, least privilege, and fail-closed authorization are what make autonomous delegation acceptable; without them the core path must not ship. It builds on Story 1.

**Independent Test**: Request consultations from a Specialist direct attempt, from a child attempt, to a Coach Agent identifier, to a Specialist outside the allowed list, to a disabled Specialist, to a Specialist whose safety policy does not permit the consultation route, beyond the per-parent bound, with a stale fencing token, with a repeated operation identifier, and after the owner lost the right to submit; confirm each answer and that no child invocation, outbox message, or waiting edge exists for a refused request.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an attempt of a direct Specialist Agent invocation or of a child consultation, **When** it requests a consultation, **Then** the request is refused as an unsupported route and nothing is created.
2. **Given** a Coach Agent attempt, **When** it requests a consultation of a Coach Agent identifier or of any identifier that is not a Specialist Agent of the catalog, **Then** the request is refused as an unsupported route and nothing is created.
3. **Given** a Coach Agent attempt whose pinned version has no consultation policy, or whose policy does not list the requested Specialist, **When** it requests that consultation, **Then** the request is refused as not allowed.
4. **Given** an allowed Specialist that is not enabled, whose enabled version's safety policy does not permit the consultation route, or whose enabled version's tool policy grants a tool outside the parent's pinned tool policy, **When** a consultation of it is requested, **Then** the request is refused with a specific reason and nothing is created.
5. **Given** a parent invocation that already has the maximum number of consultations its pinned policy allows, **When** another is requested, **Then** the request is refused as over the limit.
6. **Given** a request repeated with the same operation identifier and equivalent content, **When** the platform receives it, **Then** it returns the original consultation without creating another; with different content, it is refused as a conflict.
7. **Given** an owner who lost the right to submit for the team, **When** the parent attempt requests a consultation, **Then** the parent attempt is fenced, the parent fails as scope lost, and no child is created.
8. **Given** any request from an attempt that is not current, whose lease expired, or whose fencing token is stale, **When** it arrives, **Then** it receives the stale disposition and nothing changes.

### User Story 3 - Consultations survive restarts and fail explicitly (Priority: P3)

Hermes may stop while the Coach Agent waits, a child may fail every attempt, and the owner may lose access while children run. Waiting edges and accepted child outcomes stay in the platform; a resumed parent sees what its consultations already produced instead of asking again; a child that cannot produce an answer becomes an attributed unavailable consultation, and the parent continues or fails as its pinned policy says, never silently.

**Why this priority**: Consultations multiply the long-running work behind one question; without durable waiting and explicit unavailable outcomes, members would get answers that silently miss a consultation or never get an answer at all. It builds on Stories 1 and 2.

**Independent Test**: With the scripted provider and the simulated controls, stop the parent attempt while its child runs and confirm that the parent waits, that it is queued again once the child succeeds, and that the new attempt receives the accepted child response and completes without a second consultation; let a child exhaust its attempts with the parent's policy set to continue and to fail and confirm the unavailable consultation and the parent's outcome in each case; remove the owner's Coach role while a child runs and confirm that the parent and the child fail as scope lost.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a parent waiting for a consultation, **When** its attempt ends through lease expiry, deadline, a retryable failure, or Hermes stopping, **Then** the parent keeps its consultations and waiting edges, does not start a new attempt while any consultation is unresolved, and is queued again with a work notification once all are resolved and attempts remain.
2. **Given** a parent attempt that starts after earlier consultations were resolved, **When** Hermes claims it, **Then** the execution context lists every earlier consultation with its specialist, question, state, and accepted response or unavailable reason, and the Coach Agent synthesizes from them without consulting the same question again.
3. **Given** a child whose attempts are exhausted or that fails non-retryably, **When** it ends, **Then** its consultation is recorded as unavailable with the Specialist's attribution and the reason, and the parent's waiting edge is resolved.
4. **Given** an unavailable consultation and a pinned policy that lets the parent continue, **When** the parent synthesizes, **Then** the Coach Agent is told the consultation is unavailable and must declare it as unavailable; **Given** a pinned policy that fails the parent, **Then** the parent fails with reason "consultation unavailable" and its active attempt is ended.
5. **Given** a parent and its children, **When** the owner loses the right to submit for the team, **Then** the parent's active attempt and every active child attempt are fenced and the parent and every unresolved child fail as scope lost; no unavailable outcome is recorded for them and nothing is retried.
6. **Given** a parent that ends while a child is unresolved, **When** the parent fails, **Then** each unresolved child ends without being executed further, its later workload calls receive a stale disposition, and its consultation is recorded as ended with the parent.

### User Story 4 - Members see consultations and their influence in the Web UI (Priority: P4)

In a triage conversation, a Coach sees while a Coach Agent is consulting which Specialists it asked, and, once the advice arrives, each consultation below the Coach Agent's advice: the Specialist's name and identifier, the question the Coach Agent asked, the Specialist's response with its evidence references, whether it was unavailable, and whether the Coach Agent adopted or disagreed with it. Disagreement is shown, never smoothed over. Club Admins see and register the consultation policies that govern which Specialists a Coach Agent version may consult.

**Why this priority**: Visibility and attribution of delegated advice are what the architecture requires for consultations to be trustworthy; the platform behavior of Stories 1 to 3 must be visible to its users. It needs Story 1.

**Independent Test**: Against the simulated API, ask a Coach Agent whose answer includes an adopted, a conflicting, and an unavailable consultation and confirm the waiting and synthesizing states, every consultation's attribution, question, response, evidence, influence, conflict, and unavailable reason, and the lineage entries; in the Agent Catalog area, open and register a consultation policy and a Coach Agent version that references it; against the running platform with the Development and Test seed, confirm that the Agent Catalog area shows the seeded consultation policies referenced by the enabled Coach Agent versions.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a Coach Agent turn waiting for consultations, **When** it is shown, **Then** it states that the Coach Agent is consulting and names each requested Specialist with its state; **Given** a turn that is synthesizing, **Then** it states that the Coach Agent is combining the answers.
2. **Given** a Coach Agent advice with consultations, **When** it is shown, **Then** each consultation is listed below it, attributed to the Specialist by name and identifier, with the question, the response or the unavailable reason, the evidence references the platform returns, and the declared adoption, agreement, and note; a conflict is marked as a disagreement.
3. **Given** a lineage, **When** the owner opens it, **Then** consultation entries are worded and each consultation's own lineage can be opened.
4. **Given** the Agent Catalog area, **When** a Club Admin opens consultation policies or a Coach Agent version, **Then** the policies and the version's consultation-policy reference are shown read-only, and the Club Admin can register a consultation policy and reference it in a Coach Agent version under the area's existing registration rules.

### Edge Cases

- **Coach Agent requests several consultations at once**: Each becomes its own consultation and child, within the per-parent bound; the parent waits until all of them are resolved, and each stays independently attributed.
- **Same Specialist consulted twice**: Allowed within the bound, each with its own question; a resumed attempt receives earlier consultations and need not repeat them.
- **Consultation requested after earlier consultations resolved**: The parent moves back to waiting for consultations until the new one is resolved.
- **Specialist disabled after the consultation was created**: The child keeps the version pinned at creation and runs with it, as direct invocations do.
- **Specialist is not a participant of the conversation**: Consultation does not require it; participants govern only what the owner addresses directly.
- **Consultation question asks the Specialist to consult someone**: The child offers no consultation capability; the Specialist may only suggest further questions under "Next questions or consultations".
- **Child answers with refusals or "analysis unavailable" evidence**: It is still an answered consultation; its response is shown as returned and its evidence availability as recorded.
- **Completion without declared influence or with an unresolved consultation**: The platform refuses the completion and the attempt stays active.
- **Influence declared for a consultation of another parent**: The completion is refused.
- **Duplicate child work notifications**: They converge on the same child invocation as for direct invocations; a child never has two active attempts.
- **Child answer arrives after its parent ended**: It receives the stale disposition and is not accepted.
- **Consultation request refused**: A request refused as not allowed, unavailable, not consultable, over the limit, or invalid creates nothing; the Coach Agent receives the reason as the function's result and continues without that consultation.
- **Owner reads while the Coach Agent is still working**: The consulted Specialists, questions, and states are visible; responses and their evidence appear with the parent's outcome, because they return only to the Coach Agent.
- **Context modifiers and consultations**: A consultation's response is not a tool call of the Coach Agent's attempt and cannot back a context modifier.
- **Owner keeps only a Viewer role**: The owner can still read the conversation and its consultations, but delegation is scope lost.
- **Owner's unfinished-invocation limit**: Child invocations are created by the Coach Agent, not by the owner; they do not count toward the owner's limit of unfinished invocations, because the per-parent consultation bound limits them and their parent already counts.
- **Simulated agent workload**: The Development and Test stand-in executes child consultations like direct Specialist invocations and lets test-controlled Coach Agent invocations request consultations deterministically.
- **Invocations submitted before this feature**: Direct invocations keep their routes, pins, and behavior; Coach Agent versions without a consultation policy keep offering no consultation.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

#### Consultation policy and catalog

- **FR-001**: The agent catalog MUST support a versioned, immutable consultation policy for Coach Agents that states the Specialist Agent identifiers the Coach Agent may consult (only Specialist Agents of the catalog baseline), the maximum number of consultations per parent invocation, the maximum length of a consultation question and of the decision-support text, and whether the parent continues or fails when a consultation is unavailable; it is registered, validated, and audited like the other catalog artifacts.
- **FR-002**: A Coach Agent version MAY reference one consultation policy; a version that references one MUST have a safety policy that permits the Hermes-orchestrated `CA -> SA` route, and the reference MUST be pinned with the version's other artifacts at submission. Specialist Agent versions MUST NOT reference a consultation policy. A Coach Agent version without one MUST offer no consultation.
- **FR-003**: A Specialist Agent version MUST be consultable only when its safety policy permits the Hermes-orchestrated `CA -> SA` route.

#### Consultation request

- **FR-004**: The platform MUST let the current attempt of a direct Coach Agent invocation request a consultation with an operation identifier, its fencing token, the Specialist Agent identifier, a plain-text question, optional references to tool calls of the same attempt that answered from accepted evidence ("known evidence"), and a plain-text description of the decision support it wants, under the same current-attempt, lease, deadline, and stale-disposition rules as the other workload operations.
- **FR-005**: A valid request MUST, in one atomic step, re-evaluate the owner's right to submit for the team, record the consultation with its identifier, parent invocation, requesting attempt, Specialist, question, known evidence, and decision support, create the child logical invocation queued for the Specialist with the consultation route, pin the Specialist's currently enabled agent version and every artifact it references, record the child's inherited context (initiating owner, team, match, run, conversation, correlation, the parent's route, and the parent's pinned tool-policy reference), record the parent's waiting edge, move the parent to waiting for consultations, append lineage entries to the parent and the child, and write the child's work notification into the transactional outbox.
- **FR-006**: The platform MUST refuse a request without creating anything when it comes from an attempt whose invocation is not a direct Coach Agent invocation (including a direct Specialist invocation or a child) or names an identifier that is not a Specialist Agent of the catalog (unsupported route); when the pinned Coach Agent version has no consultation policy or the policy does not list the Specialist (not allowed); when the Specialist is not enabled (agent not available); when the Specialist's enabled version's safety policy does not permit the consultation route (not consultable); when its tool policy grants a tool outside the parent's pinned tool policy (tool policy not inherited); when the parent already has the maximum number of consultations (limit reached); or when the question or decision support is empty or too long or a known-evidence reference does not name an answered tool call of the same attempt (invalid request).
- **FR-007**: Repeating a request with the same operation identifier and equivalent content MUST return the original consultation without another change; reusing the operation identifier with different content MUST be refused as a conflict.
- **FR-008**: When the re-evaluation of FR-005 detects scope loss, the parent attempt MUST be fenced and the parent MUST fail as scope lost in the same step, exactly as for other workload operations, and no child MUST be created.
- **FR-009**: Child invocations MUST never request consultations, and no path other than the Coach Agent's request MAY create a child invocation; a consultation's child MUST NOT be addressed by members, MUST NOT appear as a turn of the conversation, and its response MUST return only to the parent.

#### Parent and child states

- **FR-010**: A parent invocation MUST be in waiting for consultations while at least one of its consultations is unresolved, and in synthesizing while it has at least one consultation, all of them are resolved, and an attempt works on it; it MUST return to waiting for consultations when a further consultation is requested. Invocations without consultations keep the states of Agent Orchestration Direct Invocations.
- **FR-011**: A child invocation MUST follow the claim, lease, checkpoint, completion, failure, expiry, retry, and stale-disposition rules of direct invocations under its own versioned child execution policy recorded on it at creation, with configured development values.
- **FR-011a**: A direct Coach Agent invocation whose pinned version references a consultation policy MUST record at submission a separate versioned execution policy for consulting parents whose maximum attempt duration covers waiting for consultations, with configured development values; every other direct invocation keeps the execution policy of Agent Orchestration Direct Invocations.
- **FR-012**: When a child succeeds, the platform MUST in the same step record its one accepted response, resolve the consultation as answered, resolve the parent's waiting edge, and append lineage entries to the parent and the child; once all consultations of the parent are resolved, the parent MUST move to synthesizing if an attempt is active, or be queued again with a work notification if none is active and attempts remain.
- **FR-013**: When a child fails because its attempts are exhausted or through any non-retryable failure (including invalid or incompatible artifacts and an unsupported route reported by the runtime), and not through scope loss or the parent ending, the platform MUST resolve its consultation as unavailable with the Specialist's attribution and the reason, and then apply the pinned consultation policy: continue (the waiting edge is resolved as for an answered consultation) or fail (the parent fails with reason "consultation unavailable", its active attempt is ended, and every other unresolved child ends with the parent).
- **FR-014**: When a parent attempt ends through lease expiry, deadline, a retryable failure, or Hermes stopping while any of its consultations is unresolved, the parent MUST keep its consultations and waiting edges and MUST NOT be claimable until all are resolved; it is then queued again while attempts remain or fails as attempts exhausted otherwise. Retries MUST NOT repeat or discard consultations.
- **FR-015**: When a parent ends (failed for any reason), every unresolved child MUST end with it: its active attempt is fenced, it fails with reason "parent ended" and is never retried, its later workload calls receive a stale disposition, and its consultation is resolved as ended with the parent.
- **FR-016**: Scope loss detected for a parent or any of its children MUST fence every active attempt of the parent and its children and fail the parent and every unresolved child as scope lost; such consultations MUST NOT be recorded as unavailable and MUST NOT let the parent continue.
- **FR-017**: The execution envelope of a parent attempt MUST list every consultation of the parent with its identifier, Specialist, Specialist version, question, state, and accepted response or unavailable reason, so that a resumed attempt can synthesize without repeating them; the execution envelope of a child attempt MUST carry the consultation's identifier, the requesting Coach Agent, the question as the current question, the decision support, the known evidence as tool and evidence references, the parent's member question, and the conversation's earlier succeeded turns as for direct invocations.
- **FR-018**: The platform MUST let the current attempt of a parent read the current state and accepted outcomes of its consultations with its fencing token, under the stale-disposition rules of the other workload reads.

#### Parent completion, influence, and lineage

- **FR-019**: The platform MUST refuse the completion of a parent attempt, without recording anything and with the attempt staying active, while any consultation of the parent is unresolved, and when the completion does not declare, for every consultation of the parent exactly once, whether its response was adopted, partially adopted, not adopted, or unavailable, whether the Coach Agent agrees with it, conflicts with it, or neither applies, and a plain-text note of 1 to 300 characters; a declaration for an unavailable consultation MUST say unavailable, and a declaration for any other consultation MUST NOT.
- **FR-020**: The accepted parent advice and the lineage MUST retain every consultation with its identifier, Specialist identity and pinned version and artifacts, question, known evidence, decision support, state, accepted child response with the child's execution references and tool-call lineage or unavailable reason, and the declared influence and agreement with its note, so that conflicts between the Coach Agent's synthesis and a Specialist's advice remain visible; earlier attempts' consultation entries are never erased.
- **FR-021**: The owner's reads of the conversation turns, the parent invocation, and its lineage MUST return the parent's consultations as in FR-020 with the parent's state; until the parent has ended, they MUST return each consultation's Specialist, question, and state but not its response, evidence, or child lineage, which become visible once the parent has succeeded or failed; the owner MUST be able to read a child invocation and its lineage under the same authorization as the parent once the parent has ended (before that, a child read answers not found); every other member MUST receive the not-found answer. Authorization MUST be re-evaluated on every read.

#### Hermes

- **FR-022**: Hermes MUST offer a Coach Agent attempt whose pinned version references a consultation policy a Hermes-local consultation function that is not an MCP tool and reads no evidence; each call requests one consultation through the platform's operation of FR-004, and the model MAY request several consultations in one turn. Hermes MUST NOT offer the function to Specialist Agents, to children, or to Coach Agent versions without a consultation policy, and MUST refuse locally a request it can see is outside the pinned policy's list or bound before calling the platform. When the platform refuses a request other than with a stale disposition, Hermes MUST return the refusal reason to the model as the function's result, record a content-free checkpoint naming the reason, and continue the attempt; stale dispositions and scope loss stop the attempt as for every other operation.
- **FR-023**: While consultations requested in a turn are unresolved, Hermes MUST keep the parent attempt alive (renewing its lease within its deadline), MUST read their outcomes through the operation of FR-018, MUST NOT count waiting parent attempts against its bound of concurrently executing attempts (a separate configured bound of waiting attempts applies), and once all are resolved MUST return to the model each consultation's attributed response or unavailable notice as the function's result.
- **FR-024**: Hermes MUST execute child invocations as Specialist invocations of the consultation route with the rules of the Hermes Agent Runtime, validating that the pinned Specialist version's safety policy permits that route, and MUST add to the rendered context a consultation section stating the requesting Coach Agent, that the answer returns only to it, the decision support, the known evidence, the member's question as context, and that the Specialist must not consult or address another agent; the consultation question MUST be the current user message.
- **FR-025**: Hermes' Coach Agent runtime section MUST, for versions with a consultation policy, state the Specialists that may be consulted, the remaining bound, that consultations must be bounded questions, that returned advice must be synthesized without hiding disagreement, and that another Coach Agent must never be consulted; for versions without one it keeps the statement that no agent may be consulted.
- **FR-026**: Before completing a parent with consultations, Hermes MUST obtain the model's influence declaration for every consultation through a Hermes-local function, validate it against FR-019, request it again at most once within the model-turn bound when it is missing or invalid, and otherwise fail the attempt as retryable with reason "advice invalid".
- **FR-027**: A parent attempt that Hermes starts after earlier consultations were resolved MUST present them from the envelope to the model as attributed consultation results, not as the model's own words, and count them against the bound.
- **FR-028**: Hermes MUST record content-free checkpoints when consultations are requested and when their outcomes are resolved, and MUST log only identifiers, the consultation and Specialist identifiers, outcomes, reason codes, counts, and durations, never questions, decision support, responses, or notes.

#### Seed and simulated workload

- **FR-029**: In Development and Test only, the platform MUST seed, once, when the catalog holds no consultation policy, a consultation policy for each of the three Coach Agents whose allowed Specialists are those of the catalog's "Typical SA consultations" column, with at most 3 consultations per parent invocation and the parent continuing when a consultation is unavailable (development values), new Coach Agent prompt template versions derived from the [First-Draft System Prompts](../../docs/architecture/intelligence-agent-catalog.md#first-draft-system-prompts) with the profile-rendering slot and with the consultation instruction restored, Coach Agent and seeded Specialist Agent safety policy versions that permit the consultation route, and new approved and enabled Coach Agent and seeded Specialist Agent versions that reference them; it MUST be skipped otherwise.
- **FR-030**: The simulated agent workload MUST execute child consultations in Development and Test like direct Specialist invocations, and MUST support a test-controlled Coach Agent behavior that requests a configured consultation, waits for its outcome, and completes with a valid influence declaration.

#### Web UI

- **FR-031**: The triage conversation views MUST word the parent states waiting for consultations (with each requested Specialist and its consultation state) and synthesizing, and MUST keep polling while the parent is in either state.
- **FR-032**: Below every Coach Agent advice with consultations, the Web UI MUST list each consultation attributed to the Specialist by name and identifier, with its question, the response with its evidence references or the worded unavailable or ended reason, and the declared adoption, agreement, and note, marking a conflict as a disagreement; all platform text MUST be rendered as text.
- **FR-033**: The lineage view MUST word the consultation lineage entries and the parent and child states, and MUST let the owner open each consultation's child lineage.
- **FR-034**: The Agent Catalog area MUST list and show consultation policies read-only (allowed Specialists, consultation bound, length limits, unavailable behavior), MUST let Club Admins register them and reference one from a Coach Agent version under its existing form, refusal, and hint rules, and MUST word the consultation route in safety policies.
- **FR-035**: All new user-facing text MUST come from translation keys with English as the only shipped language and MUST keep the views' keyboard operability, announcements, online-only rules, and automated WCAG 2.2 AA rule checks; the Web UI MUST reach the platform only through its application-owned adapters over the generated client.

#### Audit, telemetry, privacy, and interfaces

- **FR-036**: Security audit events MUST record consultation requests (created, replayed, refused with reason), child claims, completions, failures, unavailable and ended consultations, and parent failures caused by consultations, with the workload, owner, conversation, parent and child invocations, consultation, attempt, and outcome; telemetry MUST count consultations by outcome and route and report the number of waiting parents; questions, decision support, responses, notes, and advice MUST NOT appear in audit events, logs, telemetry, or work notifications.
- **FR-037**: Changed and new workload and member operations MUST appear in the platform's OpenAPI document with typed schemas and the committed OpenAPI snapshot MUST be refreshed; changed machine-readable contracts MUST receive backward-compatible releases with golden fixtures validated by the contract tests.

#### Evidence

- **FR-038**: Automated tests MUST run without model services, network access beyond locked dependency installation, or secrets, using Hermes' scripted provider, and MUST cover: the consultation policy's registration and validation, every request refusal and the route rules, idempotency and conflicts, inheritance and pinning, parent and child states, outcome delivery, unavailable consultations under both policy behaviors, parent ending and scope loss with children, restart recovery without repeated consultations, completion influence checks and refusals, lineage and owner reads, audit and telemetry content, Hermes' consultation function, waiting, child execution, influence declaration, and logs, the seed, the simulated workload, and the Web UI views against the simulated API, with a browser test against the running platform showing the seeded consultation policies in the Agent Catalog area.
- **FR-039**: An automated cross-process test MUST run the platform API, the MCP server, and Hermes as separate processes and prove that a member's question to a Coach Agent produces a consultation whose child Hermes claims and executes with at least one tool call through the MCP server, and that the Coach Agent completes with advice whose consultation, response, evidence, and declared influence are visible through the owner's reads.

### Key Entities

- **Consultation policy**: A versioned, immutable Coach Agent catalog artifact with the allowed Specialist Agents, the per-parent consultation bound, the question and decision-support length limits, and the unavailable behavior (continue or fail).
- **Consultation**: One request of a Coach Agent attempt to one Specialist Agent, identified by its consultation identifier, linking the parent invocation and requesting attempt to the child invocation, with its question, known evidence, decision support, state (pending, answered, unavailable, ended), outcome, and declared influence.
- **Child invocation**: A logical invocation of the consultation route that executes a consultation; it inherits the parent's initiating owner, team, match, run, conversation, correlation, and tool-policy context, pins the Specialist's version at creation, has its own attempts and execution policy, and returns its response only to the parent.
- **Waiting edge**: The durable link from a parent invocation to an unresolved consultation, resolved when the consultation is answered, unavailable, or ended.
- **Influence declaration**: The Coach Agent's statement, per consultation, of adoption, agreement or conflict, and a note, retained with the accepted parent advice and its lineage.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: In the cross-process test, a member's question to a Coach Agent that consults one Specialist is answered with accepted advice, its consultation, and its declared influence within 120 seconds of submission.
- **SC-002**: In automated tests, 100% of consultation requests from Specialist attempts, child attempts, or to Coach Agents, non-allowed, non-consultable, or disabled Specialists, beyond the bound, or with stale authority are refused with 0 child invocations, waiting edges, or work notifications created.
- **SC-003**: In automated tests, 100% of child invocations carry the parent's initiating owner, team, match, run, conversation, and correlation and the pins of the Specialist version enabled at creation, and 0 child responses appear as conversation turns.
- **SC-004**: In automated tests, 100% of parents interrupted while waiting complete after their consultations are resolved with 0 repeated consultations, and 100% of children that exhaust their attempts are recorded as attributed unavailable consultations followed by the outcome their parent's pinned policy prescribes.
- **SC-005**: 100% of accepted parent advice with consultations in automated tests declares the influence of every consultation exactly once, and 100% of declared conflicts are visible in the owner's reads and in the Web UI.
- **SC-006**: 0 audit events, logs, telemetry records, or work notifications in the tests contain consultation questions, decision support, responses, notes, or advice text.

## Assumptions

- **Platform and runtime authority**: Conversations, invocations, attempts, leases, stale dispositions, scope loss, retries, accepted advice, and lineage are those of [Agent Orchestration Direct Invocations](../archive/20261017-204332-agent-orchestration-invocations/spec.md); records, validation, approval, enablement, pins, and the attempt-scoped read are those of the [Agent Catalog Registry](../archive/20261018-101517-agent-catalog-registry/spec.md); direct Coach Agent execution, profile rendering, modifiers, profile checkpoints, and disclosure are those of [Coach Agent Direct Conversations](../20261018-115720-coach-agent-conversations/spec.md); execution is that of the [Hermes Agent Runtime](../20261018-113729-hermes-runtime/spec.md); tool calls, the MCP server, and their lineage are unchanged and serve child attempts as they serve direct Specialist attempts. This feature changes them only as its requirements state.
- **Allowed-Specialist list location**: The allowed Specialists, the bound, and the unavailable behavior live in a consultation policy pinned with the Coach Agent version, so they are versioned, reviewed, and pinned like the Coach Agent's other artifacts; the catalog's "Typical SA consultations" column is the provisional design input for the seed.
- **Wave 4 without its gates**: This feature implements the delegated consultation functionality of [Implementation Waves](../../docs/architecture/intelligence-agent-catalog.md#implementation-waves) Wave 4; the context inheritance, attribution, conflict preservation, loop prevention, latency, and cost gates of [Validation And Promotion Gates](../../docs/architecture/intelligence-agent-catalog.md#validation-and-promotion-gates) remain required production promotion evidence, remain open, and are not claimed. Automated tests prove behavior, not evaluation quality.
- **Configured values**: The per-parent consultation bound and length limits of the seeded policies, the consulting-parent and child execution policies, Hermes' bound of waiting attempts and outcome polling interval, and the time bound of the cross-process test are development values; production values remain open ([Production Deployment and Operations](../../docs/architecture/production-operations.md)).
- **Out of scope**: SA-initiated, `SA -> SA`, and `CA -> CA` interactions (refused), nested consultations, parallel multi-turn negotiation between a Coach Agent and a Specialist, consultations that a member requests or approves, evaluation suites and promotion gates, real provider calls in automated tests, streaming, lifecycle suppression and tombstones, and production values.
- **Evidence**: Passing tests are development evidence only and do not establish model quality, consultation quality, latency, cost, deployment support, or production readiness.
