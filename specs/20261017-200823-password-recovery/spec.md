# Feature Specification: Self-Service Password Recovery

**Feature Branch**: `20261017-200823-password-recovery`

**Created**: 2026-10-17

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "Self-Service Password Recovery: today a member who forgets the password of their local account depends on a Club Admin to issue a set-password credential; the architecture's email channel names self-service password recovery as a message type introduced \"through its own later specification\" (docs/architecture/email-notifications.md#purpose-and-scope). From the Web UI sign-in page, a member enters the email address of their account and requests a recovery; the platform always answers with the same generic confirmation and timing whether or not the address belongs to a member (no account enumeration). Only a verified email address of an active member with a local account receives a recovery message, sent through the existing transactional email outbox and SMTP delivery with minimal content; it carries a single-use, time-limited recovery link whose token is stored only as a hash, generated by the delivery worker, and carried in the URL fragment, exactly as the existing verification links (docs/architecture/email-notifications.md#email-addresses). The link opens a Web UI page where the member sets a new password that meets the existing password policy after confirming there; completing it consumes the token, ends the member's other server-validated sessions, and records security audit events (request, delivery, completion, rejection) without secrets and with masked addresses. Requests are rate-limited per address and per client; a newer request invalidates older unused tokens; expired, used, or unknown tokens fail with a neutral message. Members who must change their password at next sign-in, members linked to OpenID Connect providers, disabled members, and members without a verified address behave as the spec decides consistently with the identity architecture (recovery never bypasses provider-owned MFA and never reactivates a disabled account). Development token lifetime and rate limits are configurable values; production values stay Open / Blocking. Out of scope: invitations (spec 2), MFA, recovery by Club Admins (exists), SMS or other channels, username recovery, production SMTP relay. Depends on Member Email Addresses and Pending-Request Notifications, Club and Identity Foundation, Web UI Foundation, External OpenID Connect Sign-In (merged; archived under specs/archive/). Implements docs/architecture/email-notifications.md (account messages: self-service password recovery) and the account-recovery parts of docs/architecture/platform-implementation.md#api-and-identity and docs/architecture/security-and-data-governance.md (credential lifecycle audit)."

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-17

- Q: Should the recovery page show which account the link resets before the member sets the new password? → A: Yes: after the member opens a valid link, the page shows the account name of the link's account; checking the link this way does not use it.
- Q: How must the request answer hide whether an address belongs to a member? → A: Every answer does the same lookup and is returned no earlier than a configured minimum duration after receipt (development value 400 milliseconds); tests check that every answer takes at least that duration and that no case is consistently slower.
- Q: Should the member receive a "your password was changed" message after a successful recovery? → A: No: recovery sends only the recovery message; a separate change notice is out of scope.
- Q: Does the recovery message limit count per member or per requested address? → A: Per member: each member receives at most the configured number of recovery messages per hour, counted from the existing delivery records, so a shared address receives at most that number per member.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

The actor is any member of the club who signs in with a local password and has a verified email address. Today only a Club Admin can help a member who forgot their password, by issuing a reset credential ([Club and Identity Foundation](../archive/20261005-130701-club-identity-foundation/spec.md)). This feature adds the self-service path that [Email Notifications → Purpose And Scope](../../docs/architecture/email-notifications.md#purpose-and-scope) reserves for its own specification. It reuses the address verification, the transactional email outbox, and the delivery rules of [Member Email Addresses and Pending-Request Notifications](../archive/20261013-140140-email-notifications/spec.md), and the local-account, session, and audit rules of [API And Identity](../../docs/architecture/platform-implementation.md#api-and-identity). The Club Admin-issued reset credential stays unchanged.

### User Story 1 - Recover a forgotten password by email (Priority: P1)

A member who forgot their password opens the sign-in page, chooses "Forgot your password?", enters the email address of their account, and sends the request. The page confirms that a recovery message is on its way if the address belongs to an eligible account. The member receives a message with a recovery link, opens it, sees a page that shows their account name and asks for a new password, enters a new password that meets the password policy, and confirms. The password is replaced, every existing session of the member ends, and the member signs in with the new password.

**Why this priority**: It is the feature: it removes the dependency on a Club Admin for the most common account problem, and every other story protects this path.

**Independent Test**: With a member who has a local password and a verified address, request a recovery from the sign-in page, read the message in the development mail catcher, open its link, set a new password, and confirm that the old password is refused, the new one is accepted, and a session that was open before has ended.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the sign-in page, **When** a visitor opens it, **Then** it offers a "Forgot your password?" entry that opens a recovery request page with one field for an email address.
2. **Given** an active member with a local password and a verified address, **When** someone requests a recovery for that address, **Then** the page shows the generic confirmation and the member receives exactly one recovery message at that address.
3. **Given** a recovery message, **When** its content is inspected, **Then** it names only the kind of message (password recovery), states that it can be ignored if the member did not request it, states the link's lifetime, and contains one link to the Web UI recovery page whose token is carried only in the link's fragment; it contains no password, account name, role, team, match, player, or other club data.
4. **Given** a recovery link within its lifetime, **When** the member opens it, **Then** the recovery page shows the account name of the link's account and a form for the new password and its confirmation; opening the link alone changes nothing and does not use it.
5. **Given** the recovery page, **When** the member submits a new password that meets the existing password policy, **Then** the password is replaced, the link stops working, every session of the member ends, and the page tells the member to sign in with the new password and leads to the sign-in page.
6. **Given** the recovery page, **When** the member submits a password that does not meet the policy, or two entries that do not match, **Then** the page explains the problem, the password is unchanged, and the link keeps working.
7. **Given** a member who was required to change their password at the next sign-in, **When** they complete a recovery, **Then** the new password satisfies that requirement and the next sign-in does not ask for another change.

### User Story 2 - Recovery reveals nothing and helps only eligible accounts (Priority: P2)

Anyone can reach the recovery request page, so it must not tell whether an address belongs to a member, and it must never open an account that recovery must not open: a deactivated member, a member without a local password, or a member whose address is not verified receives nothing and the page answers exactly as for any other address.

**Why this priority**: Without it, the public request page becomes an account-enumeration and account-takeover surface. It refines Story 1's eligibility and answers.

**Independent Test**: Send recovery requests for an unknown address, for the verified address of an eligible member, for an unverified address, for the address of a deactivated member, and for the address of a member who signs in only through an external provider; confirm that all five answers are identical in status and body, each takes at least the minimum answer duration with no case consistently slower, and that only the eligible member receives a message.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** any well-formed address, **When** a recovery is requested, **Then** the answer is the same generic confirmation, whether the address is unknown, unverified, belongs to an eligible or ineligible member, or is shared by several members.
2. **Given** an address that is not well formed or is empty, **When** a recovery is requested, **Then** the page shows the field validation problem before anything is sent, independent of any account.
3. **Given** a deactivated member, **When** a recovery is requested for their address, **Then** no message is sent; **Given** a member deactivated after the message was sent, **When** the link is used, **Then** it fails with the neutral message and the account stays deactivated.
4. **Given** a member whose address is unverified, **When** a recovery is requested for that address, **Then** no message is sent.
5. **Given** a member without a local password (for example a member who signs in only through a linked external provider, or one whose set-password credential is not yet redeemed), **When** a recovery is requested for their address, **Then** no message is sent and no local password can be created through recovery.
6. **Given** a recovery link that is expired, already used, superseded by a newer request, or unknown, **When** it is opened or submitted, **Then** the page shows one neutral message that the link is not valid and offers to request a new recovery, without saying which of these cases applies.

---

### User Story 3 - Abuse limits, token lifecycle, and audit (Priority: P3)

Recovery is rate-limited so that nobody can flood a member's mailbox or guess tokens, a newer request replaces older links, any other password or address change ends open links, and every step leaves an audit trail that security reviewers can follow without seeing addresses, tokens, or passwords.

**Why this priority**: It hardens Stories 1 and 2 against abuse and makes recovery reviewable; the core flow works without it, but it must not ship without it.

**Independent Test**: Request recoveries for one member's address beyond the per-member message limit and from one client beyond the per-client limit, request twice and use the first link, change the password before using a link, and inspect the audit trail and logs for the expected events and the absence of full addresses, tokens, and passwords.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a member who received the configured number of recovery messages within the last hour, **When** another recovery is requested for their address, **Then** the answer is still the generic confirmation and that member receives no further message until the limit allows it.
2. **Given** one client exceeded the per-client request limit, **When** it requests again, **Then** it is refused with a "too many requests" answer and the time to wait, for every address alike.
3. **Given** one client exceeded the per-client limit for recovery link checks and completions, **When** it checks or submits again, **Then** it is refused with a "too many requests" answer and the time to wait.
4. **Given** two recovery requests for the same member within the per-member limit, **When** the second request is accepted, **Then** the first link stops working and only the link of the second message works.
5. **Given** an unused recovery link, **When** the member's password changes by any other means, their address is changed, removed, or becomes unverified, or the member is deactivated, **Then** the link stops working.
6. **Given** recoveries that were requested, sent, completed, and refused, **When** the audit trail is inspected, **Then** it contains one event per request, per sent recovery message, per completion, and per refused completion, with masked addresses, the affected member where one exists, and the outcome, and it, the logs, and the telemetry contain no full address, token, password, or message body.

---

### Edge Cases

- **Shared address**: Two members may verify the same address; a recovery request for it reaches each eligible member's account through its own message and link (see FR-005).
- **Request while signed in**: The request page and the recovery page work without a session; a signed-in member who opens a recovery link is treated like any other visitor, and completing it ends every session including the one in that browser.
- **Locked-out member**: A member whose local sign-in is temporarily locked after failed attempts can still request and complete a recovery (see FR-012).
- **Member with both a local password and a linked external identity**: Recovery replaces only the local password; the external link is unaffected (see FR-006).
- **Delivery fails**: A message that cannot be delivered follows the existing retry and permanent-failure rules; the requester sees nothing different, and the member can request again later.
- **Address changed between request and delivery**: The delivery worker's existing re-check discards the message when the address is no longer the one it was created for, so no link is ever issued to a previous address.
- **Link opened by an automatic scanner**: Opening the link changes nothing; only submitting the form with a new password uses it.
- **Bootstrap and break-glass**: The first Club Admin's initial password and the break-glass recovery directive stay unchanged; applying a break-glass directive ends any open recovery link of that account.
- **No email delivery configured**: Requests are still answered with the generic confirmation; messages wait in the outbox under the existing delivery rules.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

#### Request

- **FR-001**: The Web UI sign-in page MUST offer a "Forgot your password?" entry to a recovery request page where a visitor without a session enters one email address and requests a recovery.
- **FR-002**: The platform MUST answer every well-formed recovery request with the same generic confirmation, in the same form and without a timing difference that reveals whether the address belongs to a member, is verified, or belongs to an eligible member: every request MUST perform the same lookup, and no answer MUST be returned earlier than a configured minimum duration after receipt (development value 400 milliseconds). Requests with an empty or malformed address MUST fail field validation independent of any account.
- **FR-003**: A recovery message MUST be created only for a member who is active, has a local password, and whose current email address is verified and matches the requested address (compared case-insensitively after the same normalization the address feature uses).
- **FR-004**: The message request MUST be written to the existing transactional email outbox in the same unit of work as the recovery record and sent by the existing delivery worker, which MUST re-check before sending that the member is still active, still has a local password, and that the address is still their verified address, and otherwise discard the message.
- **FR-005**: When several eligible members share the requested address, each MUST receive its own message whose link applies only to that member's account.

#### Eligibility and identity rules

- **FR-006**: Recovery MUST only replace an existing local password: it MUST NOT create a local password for a member without one, MUST NOT create, change, or remove links to external identity providers, and MUST NOT bypass sign-in through a provider for members who have no local password.
- **FR-007**: Recovery MUST NOT reactivate, unlock membership of, or grant roles to any account; a deactivated member MUST receive no message, and an open link MUST stop working when its member is deactivated.

#### Link and token

- **FR-008**: Each recovery message MUST carry a single-use, time-limited link to the Web UI recovery page; the token MUST be generated by the delivery worker when it sends the message, stored only as a hash, carried only in the link's URL fragment, and never written to logs, telemetry, or audit events, exactly as the existing address-verification links.
- **FR-009**: A newer accepted recovery request for a member (one that creates a message for that member) MUST invalidate that member's older unused recovery links; a password change by any path (own change, Club Admin reset credential, break-glass directive, or recovery), a change, removal, or loss of verification of the member's address, and deactivation MUST invalidate the member's unused recovery links.
- **FR-010**: Opening the link MUST change nothing; the recovery page MUST check the token without using it and, for a valid token, show the account name of the link's account; the token MUST be used only when the member submits a new password on the recovery page.

#### Completion

- **FR-011**: The recovery page MUST accept a new password and its confirmation, MUST apply the existing password policy, and on success MUST replace the member's local password, consume the token, and end every server-validated session of the member.
- **FR-012**: Completing a recovery MUST clear the requirement to change the password at the next sign-in when it was set, and MUST NOT lift a temporary sign-in lockout before it ends.
- **FR-013**: Expired, used, superseded, invalidated, and unknown tokens MUST fail with one neutral answer that does not reveal which case applies, and a policy violation MUST leave the token usable.
- **FR-014**: After a successful completion the member MUST NOT be signed in automatically; the page MUST lead to the sign-in page.

#### Limits

- **FR-015**: Recovery messages MUST be limited per member to a configured number per hour (development value 3), counted from the existing delivery records, so an address shared by several members receives at most that number per member; requests beyond it MUST receive the generic confirmation and create no message for that member.
- **FR-016**: Recovery requests MUST be limited per client to a configured number per minute (development value 5), and recovery link checks and completions per client to a configured number per minute (development value 10); requests beyond either limit MUST be refused with a "too many requests" answer and the time to wait, for every address and token alike.
- **FR-017**: The recovery link lifetime MUST be configured (development value 60 minutes). The development and test values of FR-002 (minimum answer duration) and FR-015 to FR-017 MUST be explicit configuration; production values remain Open / Blocking.

#### Audit, privacy, and content

- **FR-018**: The platform MUST record security audit events for each recovery request (with the masked address and, for internal review only, the matched members), each sent recovery message, each completion, and each refused completion, with outcome and reason, and MUST never record a full address, token, password, or message body in audit events, logs, or telemetry.
- **FR-019**: The message MUST contain only the kind of message, a note that it can be ignored if the member did not request it, the link's lifetime, and the link; it MUST NOT contain the account name, password, roles, or any team, match, player, recording, result, or agent data, and its text MUST come from translation keys with English as the first language.
- **FR-020**: Password recovery MUST be an account message: it MUST be listed among the member's notification types as always sent and MUST NOT be possible to turn off.

#### Interfaces

- **FR-021**: The recovery request, the link check, and the completion MUST be available as anonymous `/api/v1` operations in the platform's OpenAPI document, used by the Web UI through its generated client and application-owned adapters, with the existing problem-details answers, anti-caching headers, and online-only rules; the committed OpenAPI snapshot MUST be refreshed.
- **FR-022**: The Web UI recovery request and recovery pages MUST follow the existing navigation, translation, accessibility, and problem-handling rules, and MUST be reachable without a session.

### Key Entities

- **Password recovery**: One recovery attempt for one member, created by a request that matched an eligible account. It records the member, the hashed address it was sent to, its state (pending, issued, used, superseded, invalidated, expired), the token hash once the delivery worker issues it, and when it was issued, expires, and was used. It never stores the raw token or the full address.
- **Recovery message**: An entry of the existing transactional email outbox of the password-recovery account-message type, linked to one password recovery, with the existing minimal delivery record.
- **Recovery audit event**: A security audit event of the request, issue, completion, or refused completion, with masked address, member where known, outcome, and reason.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: A member with a verified address and a local password can go from the sign-in page to signing in with a new password without help from a Club Admin in under 5 minutes, excluding mail delivery time.
- **SC-002**: In automated tests, answers to recovery requests for unknown, unverified, ineligible, and eligible addresses are identical in status and body, every answer takes at least the configured minimum duration, and no case is consistently slower than the others.
- **SC-003**: In automated tests, 100% of recovery messages go only to verified addresses of active members with a local password, and 0 messages are sent for deactivated members, unverified addresses, or members without a local password.
- **SC-004**: In automated tests, 100% of completions end every session the member had, and 0 expired, used, superseded, invalidated, or unknown links change a password.
- **SC-005**: 0 audit events, logs, or telemetry records in the tests contain a full address, recovery token, password, or message body, and every request, sent message, completion, and refused completion has an audit event.
- **SC-006**: No member receives more recovery messages per hour than the configured per-member limit, regardless of how many requests are made.

## Assumptions

- **Dependencies**: [Member Email Addresses and Pending-Request Notifications](../archive/20261013-140140-email-notifications/spec.md) (addresses, verification, outbox, delivery worker, Mailpit), [Club and Identity Foundation](../archive/20261005-130701-club-identity-foundation/spec.md) (local accounts, password policy, sessions, Club Admin reset credentials, audit), [Web UI Foundation](../archive/20261012-150657-web-ui-foundation/spec.md) (sign-in, pages, generated client), and [External OpenID Connect Sign-In](../archive/20261015-145134-oidc-sign-in/spec.md) (members linked to providers, with or without a local password), all merged.
- **Identifier**: Recovery is requested by email address; addresses stay contact data and never become a sign-in identifier. Account-name recovery is out of scope.
- **Eligibility**: Recovery serves only members who already have a local password; members who sign in only through a provider recover at their provider, and members whose set-password credential is not yet redeemed ask their Club Admin.
- **Configured values**: The link lifetime (development value 60 minutes), the minimum answer duration of a request (development value 400 milliseconds), the per-member message limit (development value 3 per hour), and the per-client request limit and link-check and completion limit (development values 5 and 10 per minute) are configuration with development and test values; production values remain Open / Blocking in [Email Notifications → Production Values](../../docs/architecture/email-notifications.md#production-values).
- **Out of scope**: Invitations by email (a later batch spec, Member Invitations by Email), a "your password was changed" notice after recovery, MFA, changes to Club Admin-issued credentials, SMS or other channels, account-name recovery, and the production SMTP relay, sender authentication, and bounce handling.
