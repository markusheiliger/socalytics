# Feature Specification: Member Invitations by Email

**Feature Branch**: `20261017-202624-member-invitations`

**Created**: 2026-10-17

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "Member Invitations by Email: today a Club Admin creates a member account and hands over a set-password credential out of band; the architecture's email channel names invitations as a message type introduced \"through its own later specification\" (docs/architecture/email-notifications.md#purpose-and-scope). A Club Admin invites a person by email address from the Web UI members area, optionally with initial team roles; the platform creates the invited member in an invited state (or the spec decides an equivalent that fits the identity model) and sends an invitation message through the existing transactional email outbox with minimal content and a single-use, time-limited invitation link whose token is stored only as a hash, generated by the delivery worker, and carried in the URL fragment, like the existing verification and spec 1's recovery links. The invitee opens the link in the Web UI, confirms, chooses an account name if the model requires it and a password meeting the existing policy, and the account becomes active with the invited address verified (receipt of the link proves control of the address); alternatively the invitee may link an OpenID Connect identity if the identity architecture allows it at acceptance. Club Admins see pending, accepted, expired, and revoked invitations, can resend (which invalidates the previous link) and revoke them; expired or revoked invitations cannot be used and the invited member never becomes active without acceptance. Inviting an address already used by a member is refused without revealing more than a Club Admin may know; security audit events record invitation, delivery, resend, revocation, acceptance, and rejection without secrets and with masked addresses. Development lifetimes and limits are configurable; production values stay Open / Blocking. Out of scope: self-registration without invitation, bulk import, invitation by Coaches, invitations to other stamps, password recovery (spec 1), production SMTP relay. Depends on specs/20261017-200823-password-recovery (batch sibling, if reused mechanics require it), Member Email Addresses and Pending-Request Notifications, Club and Identity Foundation, Web UI Foundation, External OpenID Connect Sign-In (merged; archived under specs/archive/). Implements docs/architecture/email-notifications.md (account messages: invitations), docs/architecture/terminology-and-principles.md (club users and team roles), and the member-administration parts of docs/architecture/client-applications.md#web-ui and docs/architecture/platform-implementation.md#api-and-identity."

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-17

- Q: Who chooses the account name of an invited member? → A: The Club Admin sets it when inviting, as for member creation today; the invitation reserves it, and the invitee sees it on the invitation page but cannot change it.
- Q: May the invitee accept by linking an external OpenID Connect identity instead of setting a password? → A: No: acceptance always sets a local password; a Club Admin can afterwards issue the existing link credential to the new member.
- Q: Which invitations does the Club Admin list show, and how long are ended invitations kept? → A: All invitations, newest first, filterable by state; ended invitations are kept as identity records under their existing lifecycle policy, and this feature deletes none.
- Q: Should Club Admins receive a message when an invitation is accepted? → A: No: the invitation list shows the acceptance and its audit event records it; no notification is sent.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

The actors are Club Admins, who manage club users ([Terminology](../../docs/architecture/terminology-and-principles.md#terminology)), and the people they invite. Today a Club Admin creates a member account with an account name and hands its single-use set-password or link credential to the person out of band ([Club and Identity Foundation](../archive/20261005-130701-club-identity-foundation/spec.md), [External OpenID Connect Sign-In](../archive/20261015-145134-oidc-sign-in/spec.md)). This feature adds the invitation account message that [Email Notifications → Purpose And Scope](../../docs/architecture/email-notifications.md#purpose-and-scope) reserves for its own specification. It reuses the transactional email outbox and delivery rules of [Member Email Addresses and Pending-Request Notifications](../archive/20261013-140140-email-notifications/spec.md), and the worker-issued link token, link check, per-client token limits, and public-page pattern that [Self-Service Password Recovery](../20261017-200823-password-recovery/spec.md) defines for the same channel. Creating members directly with a handed-over credential stays unchanged.

An invitation is a pending offer of membership, not a member: no member account exists for the invitee until they accept, so an invitee can never sign in, hold a session, receive other messages, or appear as an active member before acceptance.

### User Story 1 - Invite a person and have them join (Priority: P1)

A Club Admin opens the members area, chooses "Invite member", enters the person's email address, an account name, and optionally one or more team roles (Coach or Viewer per team), and sends the invitation. The person receives a message with an invitation link, opens it, sees a page that shows the club's name and the account name they are invited with, enters a password that meets the password policy, and accepts. Their member account now exists and is active, the invited address is their verified email address, they hold the invited team roles, and they can sign in.

**Why this priority**: It is the feature: it replaces the out-of-band hand-over of credentials with a verified email path, and every other story protects or administers it.

**Independent Test**: As a Club Admin, invite an address with a Coach role on one team, read the message in the development mail catcher, open its link, set a password, and confirm that the new member signs in, holds the Coach role on that team, and has the invited address as a verified address.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a Club Admin in the members area, **When** they choose "Invite member", **Then** a form asks for an email address, an account name, and optional team roles (team and Coach or Viewer), and sends the invitation on confirmation.
2. **Given** a valid invitation request, **When** it is sent, **Then** the invitation appears as pending in the Club Admin's invitation list and the invitee receives exactly one invitation message at the invited address.
3. **Given** an invitation message, **When** its content is inspected, **Then** it names only the kind of message (an invitation to join the club's SocAlytics), the club's name, the link's lifetime, a note that it can be ignored if unexpected, and one link to the Web UI invitation page whose token is carried only in the link's fragment; it contains no password, account name, role, team, match, player, or other club data.
4. **Given** an invitation link within its lifetime, **When** the invitee opens it, **Then** the page shows the club's name, the account name of the invitation, and a form for a password and its confirmation; opening the link alone changes nothing and does not use it.
5. **Given** the invitation page, **When** the invitee submits a password that meets the existing password policy, **Then** an active member account with that account name and password is created, the invited address becomes the member's verified address, the invited team roles are assigned, the link stops working, the invitation shows as accepted with a link to the new member, and the page leads the invitee to sign in.
6. **Given** the invitation page, **When** the invitee submits a password that does not meet the policy, or two entries that do not match, **Then** the page explains the problem, nothing is created, and the link keeps working.

### User Story 2 - Administer invitations (Priority: P2)

Club Admins see every invitation with its state (pending, accepted, expired, revoked), the invited address, account name and team roles, who sent it and when, when the latest link was sent and expires, and whether its latest message was sent or failed. They can resend a pending or expired invitation, which sends a new link and invalidates the previous one, and revoke a pending or expired invitation, which invalidates its link and releases its account name and address.

**Why this priority**: Invitations get lost, expire, or go to the wrong person; without resend and revoke a Club Admin cannot recover or stop an invitation. It builds on Story 1.

**Independent Test**: Invite an address, resend the invitation and confirm that only the second link works, let an invitation expire and resend it, revoke a pending invitation and confirm that its link fails, and confirm that the list shows each state.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** invitations in each state, **When** a Club Admin opens the invitation list, **Then** it lists every invitation newest first, can be filtered by state, and each invitation shows its state, invited address, account name, team roles, inviting Club Admin, creation time, latest link time and expiry, and the delivery state of its latest message (queued, sent, failed); accepted invitations link to the member they created.
2. **Given** a pending invitation, **When** a Club Admin resends it, **Then** a new message with a new link is sent, the previous link stops working, and the expiry is counted from the new link.
3. **Given** an expired invitation, **When** a Club Admin resends it, **Then** it becomes pending again with a new link.
4. **Given** a pending or expired invitation, **When** a Club Admin revokes it after confirming, **Then** it shows as revoked, its link stops working, and its account name and address are free for a new invitation or member.
5. **Given** an accepted or revoked invitation, **When** a Club Admin tries to resend or revoke it, **Then** the action is refused with a reason and nothing changes.
6. **Given** two Club Admins acting on the same invitation, **When** one resends or revokes it based on an outdated view, **Then** the action is refused as a conflict and the current state is shown.

### User Story 3 - Safe invitations: conflicts, invalid links, limits, and audit (Priority: P3)

Invitations must never create a duplicate member, never be usable after they end, never flood a mailbox, and leave an audit trail without addresses, tokens, or passwords.

**Why this priority**: It hardens Stories 1 and 2 against misuse and makes invitations reviewable; the core flow works without it, but it must not ship without it.

**Independent Test**: Invite an address already used by a member and an account name already taken, open an expired, revoked, superseded, used, and unknown link, exceed the resend limit and the per-client link limit, and inspect the audit trail and logs for the expected events and the absence of full addresses, tokens, and passwords.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an address that is the current email address of an existing member or the address of another pending or expired invitation, **When** a Club Admin invites it, **Then** the invitation is refused with "address already in use" and no message is sent.
2. **Given** an account name taken by an existing member or reserved by another pending or expired invitation, **When** a Club Admin invites with it, **Then** the invitation is refused with "account name taken".
3. **Given** an invitation link that is expired, revoked, superseded by a resend, already used, or unknown, **When** it is opened or submitted, **Then** the page shows one neutral message that the invitation link is not valid and asks the invitee to contact their club, without saying which case applies.
4. **Given** an invitation that was resent the configured number of times within the last hour, **When** a Club Admin resends it again, **Then** the resend is refused with "too many requests" and the time to wait.
5. **Given** one client exceeded the per-client limit for invitation link checks and acceptances, **When** it checks or submits again, **Then** it is refused with "too many requests" and the time to wait.
6. **Given** invitations that were created, sent, resent, revoked, accepted, and refused at acceptance, **When** the audit trail is inspected, **Then** it contains one event per creation, sent message, resend, revocation, acceptance, and refused acceptance, with masked addresses, the acting Club Admin or created member where one exists, and the outcome, and it, the logs, and the telemetry contain no full address, token, password, or message body.
7. **Given** a Coach, Viewer, Registrar, or signed-out visitor, **When** they try to create, list, resend, or revoke invitations, **Then** they are refused and nothing is revealed.

---

### Edge Cases

- **Team in an archived season at acceptance**: Teams are never deleted, so every invited team role is assigned at acceptance, as a Club Admin's direct team-role assignment would be.
- **Account name taken at acceptance**: Pending and expired invitations reserve their account name, so no other member or invitation can take it; acceptance therefore never fails for a name conflict.
- **Address used by a member after the invitation**: A member may set the invited address as their own address while the invitation is pending, because members may share addresses; acceptance still verifies the address for the new member.
- **Invitee opens the link while signed in**: The invitation page works without a session; a signed-in member who opens it is treated like any other visitor, and accepting does not change their session.
- **Inviting Club Admin loses the role or is deactivated**: The invitation stays valid; it was an action of the club, and any other Club Admin can revoke it.
- **Delivery fails**: A message that cannot be delivered follows the existing retry and permanent-failure rules; the invitation list shows the failed delivery so the Club Admin can correct it by revoking and inviting again, or resend it.
- **Link opened by an automatic scanner**: Opening the link changes nothing; only submitting the form with a password uses it.
- **No email delivery configured**: Invitations are still created; messages wait in the outbox under the existing delivery rules and the list shows them as queued.
- **Direct member creation**: Club Admins can still create members with an account name and a handed-over credential; that path neither creates nor uses invitations.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

#### Creating invitations

- **FR-001**: Club Admins, and only Club Admins, MUST be able to create an invitation from the Web UI members area with one email address, one account name chosen by the Club Admin, and zero or more team roles (one role, Coach or Viewer, per team); the invitee MUST NOT be able to change the account name.
- **FR-002**: Creating an invitation MUST validate the address with the same rules and normalization as member email addresses, the account name with the same rules as member creation, and each team role's team and role, and MUST refuse with field violations when any is invalid.
- **FR-003**: Creating an invitation MUST be refused with "address already in use" when the normalized address is the current address of any existing member (verified or not) or of another pending or expired invitation, and with "account name taken" when the account name belongs to an existing member or is reserved by another pending or expired invitation. The refusal MUST name only the conflict, which a Club Admin may know.
- **FR-004**: An invitation MUST NOT create a member account, a role assignment, or a member email address; it MUST record the invited address, account name, team roles, inviting Club Admin, and creation time, and reserve the account name and address while it is pending or expired, until it is accepted or revoked.
- **FR-005**: Creating an invitation MUST write the invitation and its invitation message into the existing transactional email outbox in one unit of work; the existing delivery worker MUST re-check before sending that the invitation is still pending and that the message belongs to its current link, and otherwise discard the message.

#### Link and token

- **FR-006**: Each invitation message MUST carry a single-use, time-limited link to the Web UI invitation page; the token MUST be generated by the delivery worker when it sends the message, stored only as a hash, carried only in the link's URL fragment, and never written to logs, telemetry, or audit events, exactly as the address-verification links and the recovery links of [Self-Service Password Recovery](../20261017-200823-password-recovery/spec.md).
- **FR-007**: Opening the link MUST change nothing; the invitation page MUST check the token without using it and, for a valid token, show the club's name and the invitation's account name; the token MUST be used only when the invitee accepts on the invitation page.
- **FR-008**: A link MUST stop working when it expires, when the invitation is resent (only the newest link works), revoked, or accepted; every such case and an unknown token MUST fail with one neutral answer that does not reveal which case applies.

#### Acceptance

- **FR-009**: Accepting MUST require a password and its confirmation that meet the existing password policy; a policy violation MUST leave the invitation and its link usable.
- **FR-010**: A successful acceptance MUST, in one unit of work, create an active member account with the invitation's account name and the chosen password, record the invited address as the member's verified email address, assign every invited team role, mark the invitation accepted with the created member, and use the token; it MUST NOT require a password change at the next sign-in, grant club roles, or link external identities. Acceptance always sets a local password; linking an external identity remains the existing Club Admin-issued link credential for the new member.
- **FR-011**: After acceptance, the Web UI MUST lead the invitee to the sign-in page.
- **FR-012**: An invitation that is not accepted MUST never result in a member account; expired and revoked invitations MUST NOT be usable.

#### Administration

- **FR-013**: Club Admins MUST be able to list all invitations, newest first and filterable by state, with their state (pending, accepted, expired, revoked), invited address, account name, team roles, inviting Club Admin, creation time, latest link time and expiry, delivery state of the latest message (queued, sent, failed), and, for accepted invitations, the created member and acceptance time. An invitation MUST show as expired once its latest link's lifetime has passed without acceptance.
- **FR-014**: Club Admins MUST be able to resend a pending or expired invitation: it MUST write a new invitation message, make the invitation pending, and invalidate the previous link and any unsent earlier message; resends MUST be limited per invitation to a configured number per hour (development value 3) and refused beyond it with "too many requests" and the time to wait.
- **FR-015**: Club Admins MUST be able to revoke a pending or expired invitation after confirming; revoking MUST invalidate its link, discard its unsent messages, and release its account name and address.
- **FR-016**: Resending and revoking MUST be refused for accepted and revoked invitations, and MUST be conditional on the invitation version the Club Admin last saw, refused as a conflict when it changed.

#### Limits and configuration

- **FR-017**: Invitation link checks and acceptances MUST be limited per client to a configured number per minute (development value 10); requests beyond it MUST be refused with "too many requests" and the time to wait, for every token alike.
- **FR-018**: The invitation link lifetime (development value 7 days), the per-invitation resend limit, and the per-client link limit MUST be explicit configuration with development and test values; production values remain Open / Blocking.

#### Audit, privacy, and content

- **FR-019**: The platform MUST record security audit events for each invitation creation, sent invitation message, resend, revocation, acceptance (with the created member), and refused acceptance, with the acting Club Admin where one exists, masked addresses, outcome, and reason, and MUST never record a full address, token, password, or message body in audit events, logs, or telemetry. Refused creations, resends, and revocations MUST be audited as refused with their reason.
- **FR-020**: The invitation message MUST contain only the kind of message, the club's name, a note that it can be ignored if unexpected, the link's lifetime, and the link; it MUST NOT contain the account name, password, roles, inviting member, or any team, match, player, recording, result, or agent data, and its text MUST come from translation keys with English as the first language.
- **FR-021**: Invitation MUST be an account message that is always sent; because it addresses a person who is not yet a member, it MUST NOT appear among members' notification preferences.

#### Interfaces

- **FR-022**: Creating, listing, resending, and revoking invitations MUST be authenticated `/api/v1` operations for Club Admins with the existing session, anti-forgery, and conditional-request rules; checking and accepting MUST be anonymous `/api/v1` operations; all MUST be in the platform's OpenAPI document, used by the Web UI through its generated client and application-owned adapters, with the existing problem-details answers, anti-caching headers for the anonymous operations, and online-only rules; the committed OpenAPI snapshot MUST be refreshed.
- **FR-023**: The Web UI MUST offer the invitation form and list in the members area for Club Admins only, and a public invitation page reachable without a session, following the existing navigation, translation, accessibility, and problem-handling rules.

### Key Entities

- **Invitation**: A pending offer of membership to one email address. It records the invited address (contact data of the identity records), the account name it reserves, the team roles to assign, the inviting Club Admin, its state (pending, accepted, expired, revoked), the token hash of its current link once the delivery worker issues it, when the current link was issued and expires, how often and when it was resent, who revoked it and when, and the member it created and when. It never stores the raw token. Ended invitations (accepted, expired, revoked) are kept as identity records under their existing lifecycle policy; this feature deletes none.
- **Invitation message**: An entry of the existing transactional email outbox of the invitation account-message type, linked to one invitation, with the existing minimal delivery record.
- **Invitation audit event**: A security audit event of creation, issue, resend, revocation, acceptance, or refused acceptance, with masked address, acting Club Admin or created member where known, outcome, and reason.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: A Club Admin can invite a person in under 1 minute, and the invitee can go from opening the message to signing in as an active member in under 5 minutes, excluding mail delivery time, without any credential handed over outside the message.
- **SC-002**: In automated tests, 100% of accepted invitations create exactly one active member with the invited account name, the invited address verified, and the invited team roles, and 0 member accounts exist for invitations that were not accepted.
- **SC-003**: In automated tests, 0 expired, revoked, superseded, used, or unknown links create a member, and every one of them gets the same neutral answer.
- **SC-004**: In automated tests, 100% of invitations for an address or account name already in use are refused before any message is sent.
- **SC-005**: 0 audit events, logs, or telemetry records in the tests contain a full address, invitation token, password, or message body, and every creation, sent message, resend, revocation, acceptance, and refused acceptance has an audit event.
- **SC-006**: No invitation is resent more often per hour than the configured limit, and only users with the Club Admin role can create, list, resend, or revoke invitations.

## Assumptions

- **Dependencies**: [Self-Service Password Recovery](../20261017-200823-password-recovery/spec.md) (batch sibling, not yet merged: the worker-issued link token, link check, per-client token limit, and public-page pattern this feature reuses), [Member Email Addresses and Pending-Request Notifications](../archive/20261013-140140-email-notifications/spec.md) (address rules, outbox, delivery worker, Mailpit), [Club and Identity Foundation](../archive/20261005-130701-club-identity-foundation/spec.md) (member creation, account names, password policy, team roles, audit), [Web UI Foundation](../archive/20261012-150657-web-ui-foundation/spec.md) (members area, pages, generated client), and [External OpenID Connect Sign-In](../archive/20261015-145134-oidc-sign-in/spec.md) (link credentials that Club Admins issue to existing members).
- **Invitation instead of invited member**: The identity model has active and deactivated members only and requires an active member for every role assignment; an invitation is therefore a separate pending record, and the member account comes into existence only at acceptance.
- **Account name**: The Club Admin chooses the account name, as for member creation today; addresses stay contact data and never become a sign-in identifier.
- **Configured values**: The invitation link lifetime (development value 7 days), the resend limit (development value 3 per invitation per hour), and the per-client link-check and acceptance limit (development value 10 per minute) are configuration with development and test values; production values remain Open / Blocking in [Email Notifications → Production Values](../../docs/architecture/email-notifications.md#production-values).
- **Out of scope**: Self-registration without an invitation, bulk import, invitations by Coaches or Registrars, club roles in invitations, accepting through an external identity provider, notifying Club Admins about accepted invitations, invitations to other stamps, editing a pending invitation (revoke and invite again), password recovery ([Self-Service Password Recovery](../20261017-200823-password-recovery/spec.md)), and the production SMTP relay, sender authentication, and bounce handling.
